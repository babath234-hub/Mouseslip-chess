HOW TO PLAY

Choose a piece and a legal destination. The piece may slip to a nearby legal destination.

If the piece has only one legal destination, it moves there normally.

Knights can only slip in the same forward/backward rank direction as the intended move.

Capturing the opposing king wins. The bot follows the same rules.

Castling is allowed. Promotion can result in a queen, rook, bishop or knight.

Online games can be rated or unrated depending on the game type.
