How to Play Mouseslip Chess

The objective

Capture your opponent's king to win.

Mouseslips

When you choose a destination, your move may slip to another eligible legal destination. Captures can also be affected.

Knights

Knights have a special mouseslip rule: alternative destinations must follow the same rank direction as the intended move.

Promotion

Pawns reaching the final rank can promote to a queen, rook, bishop or knight.

Castling

Castling is available when the relevant rights and squares allow it.

Ratings

Rated online wins give 10 points and losses remove 10 points. Private games can be rated or unrated.
